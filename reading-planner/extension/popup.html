How it works

  1. Sign in to your library website and open My Account → Checked Out.
  2. Titles and due dates are read from that page automatically (or click Sync this page now).
  3. Open the planner — your checkouts appear there with daily reading goals.

Only titles, authors and due dates are stored, on this device. No passwords.
